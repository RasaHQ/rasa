Fixed `_no_op_observe` fallback decorator (used when langfuse is not installed)
incorrectly wrapping `async` functions in a sync wrapper, causing coroutines decorated
with `@observe` to never be awaited.

Renamed the internal `rasa.builder.telemetry.langfuse` package to
`rasa.builder.telemetry.langfuse_integration` to avoid shadowing the third-party
`langfuse` package.
