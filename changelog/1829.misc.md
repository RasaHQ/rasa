Added prompts and LLM response metadata to tracker events, linking `ContextualResponseRephraser` to `BotUttered` events and `EnterpriseSearchPolicy`, `SingleStepLLMCommandGenerator`, and `MultiStepLLMCommandGenerator` to `UserUttered` events.

This behavior is enabled by setting the `RASA_RECORD_COMMANDS_AND_PROMPTS` environment variable to `true`.
