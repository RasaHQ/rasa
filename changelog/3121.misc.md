Added an in-memory guardrails strike store with user/project thresholds and block duration.

Integrated Copilot enforcement - pre-check, strike increments, immediate blocked responses.

Introduced a `GUARDRAILS_BLOCKED` response with templates, and made the feature configurable via:
- `GUARDRAILS_ENABLE_BLOCKING`
- `GUARDRAILS_USER_MAX_STRIKES`
- `GUARDRAILS_PROJECT_MAX_STRIKES`
- `GUARDRAILS_BLOCK_DURATION_SECONDS`
