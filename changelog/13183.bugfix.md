The Twilio, Twilio Voice, Mattermost, and RocketChat channel connectors now verify that an inbound webhook request originates from the respective platform (via Twilio's `X-Twilio-Signature` header and a configured `webhook_token` for Mattermost/RocketChat's outgoing-webhook token field) before processing it, matching the verification already performed by the Slack, Facebook, and Bot Framework connectors. Previously these four channels accepted and processed any request sent to their webhook URL with no authentication.
